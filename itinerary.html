A sample

What a day could look like

Two weeks at La Tierra, an hour from Chiang Mai, 6–20 January 2027. Mornings are parts work with Yaniv, afternoons are breathwork with Patricia, and evenings are circling. In the second week, you move from learning the work to leading it.

This is a sample. We read the group's energy and adjust as we go, so the details may change.

Every full day

The daily rhythm

  • 8:00–9:00Morning practice: embodiment and breath
  • 9:00–10:00Breakfast
  • 10:00–13:00Parts work: theory, then practice
  • 13:00–15:00Lunch and a two-hour break
  • 15:00–18:00Breathwork: theory, then a journey
  • 18:00–19:30Dinner
  • 19:30–21:30Circling

In the first week, mornings begin with Taoist practice to wake up the body. In the second week, participants lead the morning class.
